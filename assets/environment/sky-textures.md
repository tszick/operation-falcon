# Sky textures

Generated for Operation Falcon: Redline using the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-10. PNG assets are shipped locally and decoded once; no remote texture service runs during play.

- `cloud-cumulus-v1.png`: isolated natural cumulus cluster, transparent background, intricate vapor edges, neutral white highlights and cool gray shadows, wide 2:1 layout, no sky/terrain/text.
- `night-milky-way-v1.png`: equirectangular dark astronomical sky, tiny stars, diagonal Milky Way band and dusty lanes, restrained blue/violet tones, no terrain/moon/text.

Generation prompts:

All cloud vapor fades to full transparency with generous transparent margins at all image boundaries. Neutral illumination suitable for tinting to sunset and night in a flight simulator. High detail texture, natural photographic cloud.

Restrained brightness for a flight simulator HUD, no huge bright objects. Seamless left/right wrap, no terrain, no horizon silhouettes, no planets, no moon, no atmosphere clouds, no buildings, no text or watermark. Beautiful realistic astronomical night sky texture, not an infographic.
